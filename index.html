Concept Case StudyF&B · Hospitality

Kobi Senja — an independent coffee house, reimagined online

The brief

How should a neighborhood coffee shop with genuine craft present itself digitally — without looking like every other café template?

Design decisions

  • Editorial, magazine-like rhythm — the shop's story before its menu
  • Warm, low-saturation palette drawn from the product itself
  • One-tap WhatsApp ordering; the menu is two taps away, never more
  • Mobile-first — most customers will arrive from a phone

Delivered as concept

A complete landing experience: hero, story, menu, and ordering flow — designed to be buildable in 1–2 days.
